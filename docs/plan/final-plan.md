# FINAL MERGED PLAN — Daily English Routine (Adjudicated)

Merged from Plan A (interaction-first), Plan B (friction-minimal input engine), Plan C (periodized deliberate practice), judged against `docs/adult-language-learning.html` and `docs/METHOD.md`. This document is the deliverable; it runs as written, subject to the dated amendments at the end.

---

## PART 1 — Scores

**Plan A (interaction-first).** Evidence fidelity: strong on the interaction-beats-reception finding and prompts-over-recasts, but its ~35% interaction share plus ~20% production-adjacent work inverts the Phase 2 split (65% input / 25% interaction) for a learner who is at best entering Phase 3 — it under-supplies the input volume that the evidence says sticks (implicit g = 1.76 delayed). Adherence realism: weakest — five coordinated engagements a week, all in the tired evening anchor; its own risk #1 concedes the plan "dies quietly if evenings erode." Constraint fit: fits the anchors but rides the maximum budget daily. Measurement: good per-element predictions, honest about vocab lag. **Best exportable component:** the channel briefs plus the recast-drift monitor ("pushed to self-repair? y/n") and the scripted daily work micro-mission. **Fatal weakness:** interaction volume priced above what a tired adult will sustain in month two.

**Plan B (input engine).** Evidence fidelity: strong on input volume, narrow listening, spacing/retrieval, sleep placement — and it openly runs 18% interaction against the review's 25% Phase-2 floor and excludes 4/3/2 entirely, predicting its own primary-goal metric (wpm) stays flat for one or two cycles. For a conversation-focused learner that is a fidelity violation, not a nuance: the review's core finding is that pure-input regimes "systematically under-build the thing you actually want." Adherence realism: best of the three by a wide margin — decisions made once, autopilot playlist, the cheapest MVD, the tutor-cancellation conversion rule. Constraint fit: best (lunch anchor = listening is the only pairing that survives real workdays). Measurement: honest, including the wpm-won't-move admission. **Best exportable component:** the whole friction chassis — autopilot playlist rotation, harvest-capped SRS, standing prepaid tutor with AI-conversion rule, press-play MVD. **Fatal weakness:** knowingly under-trains speaking, deferring the fix (4/3/2) behind a two-cycle escalation gate.

**Plan C (periodized).** Evidence fidelity: the drills are the best-evidenced items in the review (HVPT g = 0.92, 4/3/2, task repetition, chunks) and the drill specs are the best-written of the three plans; but the mesocycle frame itself is imported from sport with no L2 meta-analytic support (its own admission), and "block, then interleave" is a within-session finding stretched into a 5-week state machine. Adherence realism: worst — six drill types, per-block templates, a 7-row selector, run solo by a tired person; its own risk #1 ("I don't remember what today is" → zero day) is the likely outcome. Constraint fit: totals fit, complexity doesn't. Measurement: best of the three — falsifiable per-block predictions, deload/test week, floor-vs-signal discipline. **Best exportable component:** the profile→focus mapping and the falsification table, plus the exact drill specs (4/3/2, shadow sandwich, HVPT protocol, task-repetition thread). **Fatal weakness:** complexity collapse; the plan costs willpower on the meta-level (deciding what today is) before any minute of practice happens.

## PART 2 — Skeleton decision

**Plan B's architecture hosts the merge.** Reasons, in criteria order: (1) the evidence review's own closing verdict — variance between committed learners is dominated by hours and interaction quality, not method — makes the surviving chassis the most valuable one, and B's is the only chassis built for survival; (2) adherence realism is criterion #2 and B wins it outright; (3) B's anchor mapping (morning SRS / lunch listening / evening variable) is the only one where the volatile lunch anchor holds low-stakes work. B's fatal weakness is then repaired by transplanting exactly two organs: A's interaction discipline (briefs, work missions, self-repair monitoring — at B's quota, not A's) and C's measurement rigor plus a radically simplified version of its profile selector (a one-lever "focus dial," not a block state machine). C's mesocycle scheduling is rejected; its 5-week test cadence and its drill specs are kept.

## PART 3 — THE FINAL PLAN

### 3a. Philosophy

Run a low-friction narrow-input engine on autopilot as the plan's chassis — because input volume is the best-evidenced substrate and the chassis must survive month two — but never let a week pass below the evidence floor of two real conversations (at least one human), briefed to prompt rather than recast, plus one weekly timed production drill (4/3/2), because input alone demonstrably under-builds speaking and speaking is the goal. All retrieval is spoken aloud, all new learning sits before bed, and everything the learner reaches for and lacks gets harvested into the spaced deck. The template itself never changes; every 5 weeks the diagnostic re-runs, and at most ONE lever (a slot's content, the tutor brief, or the harvest emphasis) is turned based on the named failure profile — so the routine stays automatic and the tracking data stays interpretable.

### 3b. Weekly template (Mon–Fri; totals 47–57 min)

| Day | Morning (~12′) | Lunch (10–20′) | Evening (20–35′) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | SRS reviews, spoken aloud (10) + write today's work micro-mission, one sentence (2) | Narrow listening, current topic playlist (20) | Narrow reading, same topic (15) + harvest 3–5 items into SRS, learned aloud (5) | 52′ |
| **Tue** | SRS aloud (10) + micro-mission (2) | Narrow listening (20) | **AI voice conversation**, standing brief, week's topic (20) + error harvest (5) | 57′ |
| **Wed** | SRS aloud (10) + micro-mission (2) | Narrow listening (20) | **[DIAL SLOT]** default: read-aloud/shadow sandwich on this week's transcript (15) + harvest (5) | 52′ |
| **Thu** | SRS aloud (10) + micro-mission (2) | Narrow listening (10) | Read-aloud warm-up, 3 passes of one paragraph (5) + **tutor/exchange session**, standing prepaid booking (30) | 57′ |
| **Fri** | SRS aloud (10) + micro-mission (2) | Narrow listening (15) | **Recorded 4/3/2** on a topic from the week (12) + week close: log line, playlist refill/rotation check (8) | 47′ |
| **Sat/Sun** (optional, never required) | — | — | One personal-circle conversation in English if it occurs; monthly authentic-audio check (15′); diagnostic on cycle weekends | 0–75′ |

Weekly load ≈ 265′. Shares: input ≈ 38%, spoken retrieval/study ≈ 27%, conversation ≈ 19%, other production (4/3/2, shadowing, warm-up) ≈ 14% — plus daily work-English missions uncounted. Counted interaction sits between B's 18% and the Phase-2 25% floor; the work missions and optional weekend call carry real interaction above the floor without making the schedule depend on them. Evening holds all new learning and all memory-dependent work (sleep consolidation); morning SRS retests last night's harvest after sleep — a built-in overnight spacing interval.

**Input engine mechanics (from B, unchanged):** 4 topics the learner will genuinely converse about — (a) professional field, (b) one hobby, (c) city/current events, (d) everyday life. Two active at a time; 2–3 subscribed shows per topic auto-download into one ordered playlist. Rotate a topic out when a fresh episode is ≥90% comprehensible or after ~15–20 h; rotation decisions happen in the Friday week-close only. Background/unattended audio never counts.

**SRS mechanics:** production-format only — meaning cue + source sentence with the item blanked; say the answer aloud before flipping. Seeded once in week 1 with 25 conversation-management chunks (*what I mean is…, it depends, let me put it another way, I was going to say, that's a fair point*); thereafter harvest-fed, 3–5 items/day (chunks preferred), capped ~25 new/week so morning stays ≤10′. Harvest is done from memory in the evening slot, never mid-listening; the deck runs on backlog indefinitely — an empty harvest day costs nothing. Two straight zero-harvest weeks → downgrade to one batch-harvest at Friday week-close.

### 3c. Interaction quota and channel briefs (deduplicated)

**Quota: 2 conversations/week ≥20′ is the hard floor, at least one human.** Scheduled: Tuesday AI voice (20′, zero coordination — the anxiety on-ramp) and Thursday human tutor/exchange (30′, standing recurring booking, same person, prepaid in packages). Optional third: a personal-circle call on Friday evening or the weekend, logged when it happens, never required. Daily work micro-missions are logged but do NOT count toward the quota — a quiet work week cannot break the plan. **Conversion rule:** a cancelled tutor session becomes a 20′ AI session that same evening, so the floor never breaks — but AI may substitute for the human at most one week in a row.

**One brief set, four channels:**

1. **AI voice — standing saved prompt, pasted every session:** "Voice conversation only. When I make an error, do NOT correct or reformulate me. Say 'sorry — what do you mean?' and make me repair it myself. Every ten turns, list the three errors I repeated most. Stay on [this week's topic]. Push back on my opinions so I must defend them."
2. **Tutor — sent with the booking, re-sent monthly:** "Don't reformulate my sentences. When something breaks, signal it — 'again?' — and let me self-correct. Save necessary corrections for a written list in the last 5 minutes. Each session: free conversation, then I retell one prepared story twice — interrupt the second telling only with prompts." *Focus variants (used only when the dial says so — §3e):* **decode:** "speak at natural speed, never slow down; I summarize back every few minutes, you confirm or repeat once at the same speed"; **automatize:** "first 15 min I re-do a task I've rehearsed twice this week — push me with follow-ups and time pressure"; **range:** "ban my crutch words [list]; when I use one, prompt me to paraphrase; give me the natural collocation after I've struggled, not before."
3. **Colleagues (unbriefable — convert yourself):** one scripted micro-mission per workday, written as one sentence in the morning slot — ask a real clarification question, volunteer the meeting summary, disagree once with a softener. Getting stuck mid-turn and repairing is the point. Optionally recruit one trusted colleague: "if I'm unclear, ask me to rephrase instead of guessing." Three missed missions in a week → downgrade difficulty (e.g. from "disagree" to "ask one clarification question"), never abandon.
4. **Friends/partner — said once:** "Don't finish my sentences; when I'm unclear, ask me to say it again rather than fixing it. If you notice a repeated mistake, tell me at the end, not during."

**Drift monitor (from A):** every conversation gets a "was I pushed to self-repair? y/n" mark in the Friday log. Two consecutive n's on any channel → re-paste/re-send the brief. A tutor who still recasts after two re-briefs is replaced.

### 3d. Minimum-viable day (≤15′)

Two units, attachable to whichever anchor survives:
- **Unit 1 (~7′):** SRS reviews aloud, reviews only, no new cards.
- **Unit 2 (~8′):** EITHER press play on the downloaded playlist and attend for 8 minutes, OR an 8′ AI voice conversation with the standing brief.
**Speaking-act rule:** if the previous two days contained no speaking beyond SRS, Unit 2 must be the AI option. An MVD is a full green day; two consecutive MVDs are fine; a zero day is not. Missed tutor sessions convert per §3c, never "made up" by doubling.

### 3e. Adaptation to the baseline diagnostic — the focus dial

C's block selector is kept in simplified form: **the template never changes; one dial with five positions changes at most one slot's content plus the tutor-brief variant.** Complexity survives because there is nothing to remember daily — the dial is set once per cycle, in writing, at the Friday reading of the diagnostic.

**Before the baseline exists (week 1–2):** the plan runs exactly as the table above, dial at DEFAULT. Nothing waits on the diagnostic.

**When the baseline arrives (end of week 1), map the named profile to a dial position:**

| Diagnostic profile | Dial position | What actually changes (one lever) |
|---|---|---|
| Decode-limited (dict speed ceiling < 1.2×, or authentic-audio < 85%) | **DECODE** | Wed dial slot → 15′ minimal-pair/HVPT work on contrasts taken from the learner's own dictation errors (≥4 voices, instant feedback, Friday probe on untrained words); tutor brief → decode variant |
| Knowledge–access gap or receptive–productive split (knowledge fine, wpm/ei low) | **AUTOMATIZE** | 4/3/2 goes twice-weekly (Wed dial slot becomes the second run); the Thursday tutor retell becomes attempt 3 of a task rehearsed Tue (AI) and Wed; tutor brief → automatize variant |
| Passive vocabulary or fluent-but-thin (vocab high / beyond_core or MTLD low) | **RANGE** | Harvest raised to 5/day with forced-use: each harvested item must appear in the next AI session; tutor brief → range variant (crutch-word ban) |
| Use-constrained (test metrics fine, evidence_n low) | **USE** | Work micro-missions upgraded in stakes; the optional third conversation becomes expected-and-logged |
| Flat / no dominant weakness | **DEFAULT** | Template as written, no changes |

**Cycle rule:** re-run the diagnostic every 5 weeks (weekend, rested — the diagnostic weekend's weekdays run as MVDs Mon–Wed, which also rehearses the fallback). Read the delta table against noise thresholds; move the dial only if the current position's target metric failed to move ≥ threshold, and turn **at most one lever per cycle**. A dial position that fails twice consecutively (10 weeks) is abandoned for the next-priority profile. Priority order when several profiles apply: decode → automatize → range → use (perception is upstream of everything).

### 3f. Measurement hooks

**Instruments and cadence:** `diagnostic.html` in week 1 (baseline, battery v2 — v1 non-comparable) and every 5 weeks after, same conditions, row appended to `tracking.tsv`. Monthly: authentic-audio check on a never-heard episode from a rotated-out or untrained topic (doubles as the narrow-listening transfer test); log the real-speech penalty vs synthetic dictation. Quarterly: free EF SET as external anchor. Weekly telemetry (Friday log line, 2′): conversations completed (floor 2), self-repair y/n per session, missions done /5, 4/3/2 done y/n + rough wpm from the recorded 2-minute telling, harvest count.

**Per-element predictions (testable despite the merge):**

| Element (constant from week 2) | Should move | Should NOT move |
|---|---|---|
| Lunch input engine + harvest SRS | `vocab_size`, `dict_pct` first; `ctest_pct` next | `wpm` |
| Weekly 4/3/2 + spoken SRS + 2 conversations | `wpm` (likely inside ±12 band in cycle 1; ≥+12 by cycle 3 or the AUTOMATIZE dial fires), filler ratio down | `mtld` |
| Chunk SRS + tutor retell (task repetition) | `ei_pct` (softest number — never decides anything alone) | — |
| Shadow sandwich | `dict_pct`, authentic-audio penalty shrinking | `vocab_size` |
| Missions + conversations | `evidence_n` — should saturate fastest; confirms the architecture is running | — |
| MTLD / `beyond_core_pct` | expected to LAG unless the RANGE dial is set — this is the merged plan's declared cost | |

**Attribution discipline — the merge problem handled explicitly:** because the merged plan starts many elements simultaneously, **cycle-1 deltas are attributed to "the plan as a whole," never to elements.** From cycle 2 onward, exactly one lever changes per cycle, so between-cycle deltas attribute to that lever; the constant elements' predictions above are tested on their cumulative trajectory across cycles, with the weekly 4/3/2 wpm spot-check and (when DECODE is set) the Friday untrained-word probe providing within-cycle telemetry that doesn't wait for the battery. Only movement at/above noise thresholds counts (C-test ±8, vocab ±1,100 families, dictation ±10, wpm ±12, MTLD ±8); repeat scores are floors — upward signals are trustworthy, flat ones ambiguous, and the monthly authentic-audio check (novel material every time) is the tiebreaker for the decode domain.

### 3g. Onboarding, weeks 1–2

**Week 1 — stand up the engine, speak once, then measure:**
- **Day 1 (evening, ~30′ one-time):** choose the 4 topics; subscribe 2–3 shows per active topic; build the auto-download playlist; save the AI standing prompt.
- **Day 2 (evening, ~25′):** create the SRS deck (production-aloud template), seed 25 conversation-management chunks; then do a first 10′ AI conversation — speaking starts in week one; the delay doctrine is the weakest-supported claim in the review.
- **Day 3:** book the standing Thursday tutor slot — recurring, same person, prepaid if possible — with the base brief pasted into the booking message.
- **Days 2–5:** run only morning SRS + lunch listening (~35′/day) to set the anchor habit before adding load.
- **Weekend:** run `diagnostic.html` (~60′, rested), copy the row to `tracking.tsv`; save the speaking transcript and dictation error list (raw material for HVPT contrasts and crutch words). Read the profile, set the dial position in writing, and put the 5-week re-run in the calendar now.

**Week 2 — full template live, at the dial position the baseline chose:**
- Mon: first harvest + first before-bed learning session. Tue: first briefed AI session on the week's topic. Wed: first dial-slot session (sandwich, or HVPT if DECODE fired). Thu: first tutor session — deliver the brief verbatim in the first two minutes; run the retell task. Fri: first recorded 4/3/2 (archive the recording — it becomes the longitudinal artifact) + first week-close log line; confirm ≥5 episodes queued; if lunch comprehension is below ~80–90%, switch that topic's sources to learner-oriented content or add transcripts.

### 3h. Top 3 adherence risks of the MERGED plan

1. **Input crowds out interaction (created by the merge).** Neither parent had this failure: A was all-interaction, B accepted low interaction *by design*. The merged plan lets a learner feel fully adherent — most minutes green, playlist consumed, SRS cleared — while quietly dodging both conversations, reproducing the immersion production gap inside a plan that thinks it fixed it. *Mitigation:* the week is scored by conversations completed, not minutes — it is the first field of the Friday log; the cancelled-tutor→same-evening-AI conversion keeps the floor mechanical; two consecutive weeks below 2 conversations auto-downgrades Thursday to "20′ AI, non-negotiable" (a kept-small floor beats a broken-large one) and flags the next cycle's dial reading.
2. **Evening stacking → tutor-cancellation spiral.** The merge concentrates all three high-value evening events (AI Tue, tutor Thu, 4/3/2 Fri) in the tired anchor, and the tutor is the only appointment — each cancellation makes the next easier, and anxiety pushes the same direction. *Mitigation:* standing prepaid recurring slot with the same person (social + financial cancellation cost); the conversion rule caps damage at one AI-substituted week; 4/3/2 may shift to a Saturday-morning voice memo without penalty; the MVD counts as a full green day so a bad Tuesday doesn't cascade into a zero week.
3. **Dial fiddling destroys both the routine and the data (created by the merge).** Having a selector at all invites turning it — new cycle, three levers "obviously" worth changing, and suddenly the template isn't automatic and no delta is attributable to anything. *Mitigation:* lever changes are legal only during the diagnostic week-close, only one per cycle, only when the current target metric failed its threshold, and each change is written as one line in `tracking.tsv`'s notes; any confusion about "what is today" resolves to the DEFAULT template — the printed week table is the only artifact the learner ever needs to consult.
