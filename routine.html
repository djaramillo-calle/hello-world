Terrible day? ≤15 minutes keeps the chain

  • ~7′ SRS reviews aloud — reviews only, no new cards.
  • ~8′ Press play and attend, or an 8′ AI conversation.
  • Rule: two days with no speaking beyond SRS → the AI option is mandatory.

An MVD is a full green day. Two in a row is fine. A zero day is not.

The five standing rules

  1. 2 conversations/wk is the floor, ≥1 human. Cancelled tutor → 20′ AI the same evening. AI substitutes at most one week in a row.
  2. The week is scored by conversations completed, not minutes.
  3. Background audio never counts. Attended only.
  4. All new learning sits in the evening slot — sleep does the consolidation.
  5. Missed sessions are skipped or converted, never doubled.

Briefs — how everyone should push you

Prompts beat recasts: the interlocutor signals the breakdown and you repair it. Log "pushed to self-repair? y/n" per session; two n's in a row on any channel → re-send the brief.

AI voice — paste every session

Voice conversation only. When I make an error, do NOT correct or reformulate me. Say "sorry — what do you mean?" and make me repair it myself. Every ten turns, list the three errors I repeated most. Stay on [this week's topic]. Push back on my opinions so I must defend them.

Tutor — send with booking, re-send monthly

Don't reformulate my sentences. When something breaks, signal it — "again?" — and let me self-correct. Save necessary corrections for a written list in the last 5 minutes. Each session: free conversation, then I retell one prepared story twice — interrupt the second telling only with prompts.

Friends — said once

Don't finish my sentences; when I'm unclear, ask me to say it again rather than fixing it. If you notice a repeated mistake, tell me at the end, not during.

Every 5 weeks

Mon–Wed of diagnostic week: MVDs only (rehearses the fallback). Weekend, rested: run the Signal Check diagnostic, append the row to tracking.tsv. Read the delta against noise thresholds. Move the dial only if the current position's target metric failed its threshold — and turn at most one lever per cycle, in writing. Monthly: authentic-audio check on a never-heard episode. Quarterly: EF SET anchor.

Why this plan — the adjudication in one paragraph

Three independent designs (interaction-first, friction-minimal input engine, periodized deliberate practice) were merged by an adjudicator against the evidence review. The input engine's chassis won because adherence is the binding constraint; its known weakness — under-trained speaking — was repaired with the interaction plan's briefs and drift monitor at a sustainable 2-conversation floor, plus an immediate weekly 4/3/2 (the interaction plan's work missions were also adopted, then removed at the user's request on 2026-08-30). The periodized plan's 5-week mesocycle state machine was rejected as unevidenced for L2, but its diagnostic-driven adaptation survives as the one-lever focus dial, and its measurement discipline is kept whole: cycle-1 changes attribute to the plan as a whole, later changes to the single lever turned.
