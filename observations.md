# English observation log

Authentic-production observations harvested from working chats. Feeds the
SRS deck, the tutor crutch-word list, and (as secondary evidence only) the
focus dial. **Never** a scored metric — nothing here enters `tracking.tsv`.

Rules (see CLAUDE.md):
- A pattern needs **≥2 independent occurrences**; single slips are presumed
  typing noise and go to *watching* status.
- Written chat ≠ speech: register is technical/instructional, self-paced.
  No conclusions about real-time speaking follow from this log.
- No unsolicited mid-chat corrections; digests are weekly or on request.

Status legend: `WATCHING` (1 occurrence) · `PATTERN` (2+, card-worthy) ·
`STRENGTH` · `PROMOTED` (moved into SRS/tutor brief) · `RETIRED` (no
recurrence across 4+ weeks).

## 2026-08-30 — initial entries (session 1)

### PROMOTED — article omission with abstract/deverbal nouns
Classic L1-Spanish transfer. Evidence:
- "launch **∅** double blind audit of the tool" → *launch **a** double-blind audit*
- "goal is conversation" → *the goal is conversation*
- "find the correct space for **the** activities" (article present here — the
  pattern is omission before abstract nouns, not blanket loss)

Suggested cards (production-format, spoken):
- "We need to run ___ full audit before release." → **a**
- "___ goal of this phase is fluency." → **The**

*Promoted 2026-08-30: both cards are in `seed-deck.csv` (rows 26–27).
Still an open pattern in production — watch for recurrence to gauge RETIRED.*

### PROMOTED — phrasal-verb avoidance (formal-register monoculture)
Near-zero phrasal verbs across all messages; consistently Latinate:
*launch, incorporate, remove, identify, implement, evaluate, recommend*.
Grammatical — but in conversation this register reads stiff, and the
review names formulaic/phrasal chunks as the engine of fluent speech.

Suggested chunk cards: *set up* (≈ implement/establish), *find out*
(≈ identify/determine), *come up with* (≈ generate/devise), *sort out*
(≈ resolve), *work out* (≈ calculate/figure out), *build in* (≈ incorporate).
Tutor note: prompt paraphrase when only Latinate verbs appear in speech.

*Promoted 2026-08-30: all six chunk cards are in `seed-deck.csv`
(rows 28–33). Tutor note carries forward to the next brief refresh.*

### WATCHING — subject–verb agreement
- "our chat **are** a source" → *our chats are* / *our chat is*.
One occurrence; plausibly a typo. Promote on recurrence.

### WATCHING — uncountable *time*
- "consuming available **times**" → *using the available time*.
One occurrence.

### WATCHING — demonstrative reduction
- "explain **is** item" → *explain this item*. Presumed typo (th- dropped);
watch for th-demonstrative slips in speech transcripts, where it would
matter more.

### STRENGTH — technical/academic collocation
- "incorporate language learning into my daily routine"
- "mapped to specific domains"
- "minimum effective time"
All natural and precise. Lexical-grammatical base is solid; the leaks are
small function words, not content structure.

### Register note
All evidence is from instructional/technical requests. Past-tense
narrative, social softeners, and spoken repair are **unsampled** — expect
those gaps to surface in the diagnostic's speaking module, not here.

the same measurement. Flagged for the Friday review; nothing changed today.
